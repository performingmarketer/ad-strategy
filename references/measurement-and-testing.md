# Measurement and Creative Testing

## Core formulas

- Link CTR = link clicks / impressions × 100. Identify whether you are using link CTR or all-click CTR.
- CPC (link) = ad spend / link clicks.
- CPM = ad spend / impressions × 1,000.
- CPL = ad spend / leads.
- Qualified lead rate = qualified leads / all leads × 100.
- Cost per qualified lead = ad spend / qualified leads.
- Conversion rate = desired conversions / defined eligible visits or clicks × 100. State the denominator.
- CPA = ad spend / attributable acquired customers or paid admissions.
- ROAS = attributable revenue / ad spend; revenue is not profit.
- Appointment-to-sale rate = customers / attended appointments × 100.

Do not fabricate benchmarks, customer counts, conversion events or results.

## Budget-Tier Creative Testing Guide (Illustrative Starting Points)

Adapt testing volume to budget and conversion volume rather than over-fragmenting spend:

| Monthly Budget Tier | Active Concepts to Test | Recommended Approach | Review Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter (₹5,000 – ₹15,000/mo)** | 2–3 distinct concepts | 1 campaign, focused objective (e.g., WhatsApp or Leads). Avoid splitting budget across too many ad sets. | Weekly review of lead quality & actual enquiries |
| **Growth (₹20,000 – ₹50,000/mo)** | 4–6 distinct concepts | Separate proven winners from new creative tests when conversion volume supports it. | Weekly creative signal review + bi-weekly iteration |
| **Scale (₹1,00,000+/mo)** | 6–10+ concepts across funnel stages | Structured testing (including supported A/B tests for key hypotheses) + dedicated retargeting/objection-handling layer. | Bi-weekly cohort/CPA review + continuous creative refresh |

## Interpret results carefully

| Symptom | Check first | Possible creative action |
|---|---|---|
| Low link CTR | Message clarity, relevance, visuals, placement | Test a stronger problem/benefit hook |
| High link CTR, poor conversions | Landing-page speed, promise/offer alignment, visitor quality | Align ad and destination; qualify users |
| Low CPL, low lead quality | Form friction, message accuracy, follow-up | Test transparent pricing or qualification |
| High CPM | Competition, audience restrictions, seasonal auction | Assess contextual factors; do not blame creative automatically |
| Declining CTR and rising frequency | Time trend, audience size and demand | Test a fresh concept if fatigue is plausible |
| More enquiries, fewer sales | Sales process, objections, offer fit | Test objection-handling and proof content |

## Testing practices

- Define a business objective and a hypothesis for each concept.
- Keep reporting windows, definitions, objectives and attribution settings comparable.
- Start with 2–3 differentiated concepts for small budgets rather than many competing ad sets.
- Meta's delivery allocation can be uneven. An ad getting more budget is not proof it is intrinsically better.
- Use supported A/B experiments to isolate a variable when feasible; routine delivery comparisons are directional.
- Do not make a rigid 3-day or 7-day winner rule. Consider spend, data volume, seasonality and observed conversions.
- Avoid unnecessary edits and repeated learning resets.

## Lead-generation tracking

Track creative ID, ad ID, campaign, enquiry date, lead source, qualification status, appointment/counselling, outcome, revenue, and follow-up status, subject to consent and privacy rules. Prefer verified business results over vanity metrics.
