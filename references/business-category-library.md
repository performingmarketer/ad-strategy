# Business Category Library — Ad Strategy v1.1

**Purpose:** Industry-specific starting points for a *universal* Meta Ads creative strategy. These are marketing-planning categories, **not Meta's Special Ad Categories** or verified targeting interests. The skill must work with any business, including industries not listed here.

## Category-selection logic

1. Ask for the business category only if the user has not already supplied it; ask for the specific product/service, not just the broad industry.
2. Match to the closest category below, allowing hybrids (e.g., "salon academy" = beauty + education) and niche businesses. Do not force an exact match; generate a custom category when missing.
3. Use the category entry to inspire **potential** buyer motivations, candidate creative concepts, suitable authentic proof, and the business conversion funnel. These are hypotheses until supported by customer evidence.
4. Tailor separately by product, business model, location, local culture, budget, buyer decision stage, and conversion objective. Avoid copying the same hooks across industries.
5. Distinguish a general **industry category** from ad account **Special Ad Categories**. Check current Meta policies before claiming eligibility, targeting permissions, or category restrictions.
6. Do not ask the user to choose from a massive menu by default. If unclear, give at most 4 relevant possibilities or accept their free-text description.

## Industry-to-creative guidance

### Education and training
- Angles: choosing the right course, real learning experience, instructor explains a concept, actual curriculum/projects, cost and schedule clarity, online/offline comparison, genuinely consenting student experiences.
- Resist job/income guarantees, artificial urgency and invented results. Prioritize **actual registrations/admissions** over low-cost leads.

### Health and wellness
- Angles: educational explainer, service process, facility tour, appointment logistics, practitioner qualifications, realistic expectations and aftercare information where appropriate.
- Avoid implying the viewer has a health condition, unfounded treatment claims, sensational before/after imagery, and restricted targeting. Review local medical advertising and Meta rules.

### Retail and ecommerce
- Angles: real unboxing/demo, quality and fit, side-by-side feature guide, real-life use case, authentic reviews, actual shipping and returns, bundle value and honest offers.
- Track purchases, profit contribution and returns, not clicks alone.

### Food, travel and hospitality
- Angles: authentic experience, product/room walk-through, behind-the-scenes, occasion use cases, actual venue/location, FAQ, verified prices and availability.
- Track completed booking/visit/order and revenue where available, not just messages.

### Housing, property and professional regulated sectors
- Prioritize accurate listing/project documentation and fair availability claims; check applicable Housing, Employment and Credit restrictions for Meta campaigns where relevant.
- Financial, legal and health services require truthful qualifications, suitable disclosures and extra attention to applicable local laws and Meta policies. Do not claim a special category automatically applies without checking current rules.

### B2B, SaaS and industrial
- Angles: operational problem, demo, cost-of-inaction framing (without invented savings), implementation steps, evaluation checklist, verifiable case study, specifications and procurement FAQ.
- Track qualified demo/RFQ/consultation → sales pipeline → signed deal, with longer purchase cycle.

### Local appointment businesses
- Angles: service explanation, staff/team introduction, credible process, local availability, pricing transparency, genuine customer feedback and booking walkthrough.
- Track booked and attended appointments or completed jobs, not just incoming messages.

## Creative diversity QA across categories

For any selected business, propose different reasons to choose, not simply different visual treatments. At least three of the following should differ across the lead creative set: buying motivation, primary buyer question, demonstrated product/service benefit, type of proof, use case, buying stage, or objection.

When facts are unknown, label proposed hooks and customer objections **hypotheses**; never present sample testimonials, clinical results, savings, discounts, job placements, product stock levels, prices or regulatory credentials as real.
