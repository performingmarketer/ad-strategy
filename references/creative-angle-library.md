# Creative Angle Library

Creative diversity means distinct buying motivations, decision barriers, proofs and messages—not simply recoloring the same ad.

## Creative concept families

| Angle | Customer question | Evidence/visual | Primary goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem awareness | Is this a problem worth solving? | Relatable scenario, diagnosis | Relevant attention |
| Solution demonstration | How does it work? | Actual demonstration | Comprehension |
| Educational | What should I know before choosing? | Checklist, explainer, tutorial | Trust |
| Proof / testimonial | Can I trust this? | Authentic customer experience (permission required) | Credibility |
| Expert credibility | Who is delivering the service? | Real expert, verified credentials | Reassurance |
| Value breakdown | What is included? | Accurate deliverables, pricing | Informed comparison |
| Objection handling | What about price, time, quality or fit? | Transparent FAQ | Qualified conversion |
| Comparison / decision aid | Which option suits me? | Fair, fact-based comparison | Consideration |
| Behind the scenes | What is the real process? | Genuine work, staff, facilities | Trust |
| Lifestyle / use case | Where does this fit in my life? | Realistic usage scenes | Relevance |
| Offer / availability | Why act now? | Verified price, availability or deadline | Action |
| Customer journey | What happens after I enquire? | Real service workflow | Reduced friction |

## Hook Formulas Library (Malayalam & English Patterns)

Use these patterns to build fast, relevant, non-clickbait openings (adapt naturally to the business and audience):

1. **Mistake / Warning Hook:** Call out a common buying mistake.
   - *ML:* "[Product/Service] എടുക്കുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് ഈ ഒരു അബദ്ധം പറ്റരുത്!"
   - *EN:* "Don't make this common mistake before choosing a [Product/Service]."
2. **Checklist / Decision-Aid Hook:** Position the brand as a helpful guide.
   - *ML:* "[Product/Service] തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുമ്പോൾ നിർബന്ധമായും നോക്കേണ്ട 3 കാര്യങ്ങൾ!"
   - *EN:* "3 things to check before you pay for [Product/Service]."
3. **Myth-Busting Hook:** Challenge a widespread assumption.
   - *ML:* "എല്ലാവരും കരുതുന്നത് [Common Myth] എന്നാണ്... പക്ഷേ സത്യം ഇതാണ്!"
   - *EN:* "Most people think [Common Myth]—here is what actually matters."
4. **Price / Value Transparency Hook:** Filter for serious buyers upfront.
   - *ML:* "എന്തുകൊണ്ടാണ് [Cheap Option]-നേക്കാൾ [Your Product/Service]-ന് വില അല്പം കൂടുന്നത്?"
   - *EN:* "Why does [Your Product/Service] cost [Price] when cheaper options exist?"
5. **Process / Behind-the-Scenes Hook:** Build instant credibility with real proof.
   - *ML:* "ഒരു [Service/Order] ഞങ്ങൾ എങ്ങനെയാണ് കംപ്ലീറ്റ് ചെയ്യുന്നത് എന്ന് നേരിട്ട് കാണാം."
   - *EN:* "Here is exactly what happens step-by-step when you book with us."
6. **Direct Qualification Hook:** Speak directly to a specific use case (avoid asserting sensitive personal attributes).
   - *ML:* "[Location]-ൽ [Specific Need] അന്വേഷിക്കുന്നവർക്കായി ഒരു ചെറിയ അപ്‌ഡേറ്റ്."
   - *EN:* "Looking for [Specific Need] in [Location]? Watch this before you enquire."

## By business type

- **Education**: Curriculum, instructor expertise, practical classes, projects, flexible attendance, genuine student experiences, cost and admissions.
- **Local services**: Process walkthrough, quote transparency, service quality, verified reviews, local trust, appointment steps.
- **E-commerce**: Product demo, specifications, sizing, use cases, real customer feedback, genuine comparisons and delivery.
- **Restaurants/hospitality**: Authentic food/property footage, menu/amenities, experience, verified offers, guest reviews and booking.
- **Real estate**: Accurate property tour, location, price transparency, financing information, verified facilities and site visits.
- **Healthcare**: General health education, qualified practitioner information, treatment process and appointment information; no exploitative personal-attribute messaging.
- **B2B**: Workflow efficiency, demos, integrations, buyer objections, verified case studies and qualified meeting booking.

## Diversity validation

Before output, compare each pair of concepts: target motivation, core promise, actual proof and customer objection. Replace a concept if it is substantially the same as another. Test one meaningful creative hypothesis at a time when practical; avoid promising equal delivery.
