# Meta Advertising, Trust and Accuracy Checklist

This is an educational checklist, not legal advice. Meta Ads policies and product controls evolve; review the current official policies and account-specific settings before launch.

1. Never invent testimonials, ratings, trainer credentials, price cuts, deadlines, stock levels, job placement results, revenue or return on ad spend.
2. Avoid copy that directly asserts a viewer's sensitive or personal attributes (health conditions, financial situation, religion, age, etc.). Use general service/education framing.
3. Verify whether an applicable Meta Special Ad Category is required, including for relevant housing, employment, and financial-product promotions, and apply available restrictions where applicable.
4. Healthcare, finance and other regulated industries may require extra review, evidence and compliance checks.
5. For education, distinguish opportunities or career-support services from guaranteed employment or salary claims. Internship availability and course pricing must be factual.
6. Do not suggest that a business is officially endorsed by Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, Facebook or Instagram without evidence.
7. Use images, videos and customer statements only when owned, appropriately licensed or authorized.
8. Ads must match the destination's actual offer, availability, prices, and services.
9. Use genuine reviews and customer permission for identifiable endorsements. Do not fabricate before-and-after results.
10. Respect privacy, permissions and applicable messaging rules when recording leads or following up by WhatsApp.
11. Do not guarantee approval, fixed lead cost, revenue, admissions or ad delivery.
12. Distinguish proposed hypotheses from observed metrics, and verify current platform features with official sources where possible.
