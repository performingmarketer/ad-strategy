# Ad Strategy Output Templates

## Full Strategy
1. Business snapshot, known facts, assumptions and evidence gaps
2. Business objective and conversion funnel
3. Customer psychology matrix: segment, problem, motivation, objection, required proof, message
4. Unique positioning and offer
5. 8–10 distinct creative concepts with hook, format, visual, proof and CTA
6. Five prioritized ad packages (or a concise summary if requested)
7. Budget-conscious creative testing plan
8. Measurement KPIs and attribution limitations
9. 30-day production and review roadmap
10. Policy and factual verification checklist

## Quick Strategy
Business snapshot; three customer insights; five differentiated concepts; two production-ready assets; recommended first test; primary KPI.

## Single Asset
Requested format only (e.g. 60-second Malayalam Reel): audience; motivation; 2–3 hooks; complete script; optional scene plan; primary text/headline/CTA when useful. Do not generate an unrelated long report.

## Review / Optimize
Separate provided results from assumptions. Audit customer journey and metric definitions; identify competing explanations; prioritize the next controlled or directional tests. Never invent data.

## Individual concept schema
- **Title / target segment**
- **Customer motivation and pain point**
- **Unique claim and required proof**
- **Three hooks**
- **Recommended format and visual sequence**
- **Natural spoken script** (when requested)
- **Primary texts, headlines and CTA**
- **Testing hypothesis, leading metric and verified business outcome**

## Language
Use the user's language with natural local market terminology. For Malayalam, retain English advertising terminology where that improves comprehension. Avoid overly dense scripts or mechanically translated claims.
