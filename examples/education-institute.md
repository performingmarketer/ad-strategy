# Example: Digital Marketing Training Institute (Illustrative)

## Known inputs
Business: Performing Marketer. Service: digital marketing course. Location: Thrissur District, Kerala. Delivery: hybrid. Course fee: ₹40,000 (online/offline differences not verified). Audience: students/beginners and business owners. Monthly Meta budget: ₹5,000–₹10,000. Conversion goal: confirmed admissions via WhatsApp.

Claimed course features to verify before publication: practical training, live projects, AI-powered learning, expert mentors, SEO, organic marketing, Google Ads, Meta Ads, website development, flexible batches, career guidance, internship opportunities.

## Two distinct audience paths

| Segment | Motivation hypothesis | Ad angle | Conversion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students / beginners | Build practical skills | Practical campaign demo | Qualified WhatsApp enquiry → counselling → paid admission |
| Students comparing courses | Make an informed choice | 3 checks before selecting a course | Enquiry quality and enrolment |
| Business owners | Understand marketing decisions | How to interpret ad reports | Business-owner consultations → admissions |
| Hybrid learners | Fit training into schedules | Online/offline and batch options | Questions answered and admissions |

## Five example concepts

1. **Course Selection Checklist**: “Digital Marketing Course തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് ഈ മൂന്ന് കാര്യങ്ങൾ പരിശോധിക്കൂ!” Teach viewers to ask about practice, real projects and mentors.
2. **Hands-on Demo**: Show a *real* dashboard lesson or classroom project (redact client data). Hook: “Meta Ads പഠിക്കുമ്പോൾ Actual Campaign Setup കാണേണ്ടത് എന്തുകൊണ്ട്?”
3. **Business Owner Ad Literacy**: Hook: “Agency Report കിട്ടുമ്പോൾ Business Owner എന്തൊക്കെ പരിശോധിക്കണം?” Explain basic conversion indicators.
4. **AI + Marketing Reasoning**: Show AI generating draft copy, followed by customer-research, proof and budget checks by a marketer.
5. **Transparent Course Value**: Explain verified curriculum and fee with no job guarantees; confirm ₹40,000 applies to the advertised training format.

## Recommended first test
One practical WhatsApp enquiry campaign where supported by the account, 2 contrasting creative concepts initially, a clear lead qualification flow and weekly review of *actual admissions*. Uneven ad delivery makes comparisons directional rather than strictly causal.

## Compliance
Do not promise job placement, income or admissions. Verify internship terms, fee applicability, real-project permissions, and use of identifiable student images/testimonials.
